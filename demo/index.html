The problem

It's 9 pm. A mother fills in your Request a Visit form. Tomorrow morning, will someone call her? How soon? Would anyone know if nobody did?

In most preschools, follow-up depends on someone remembering. Parents wait without a reply, head office can't see response times across centres, and enquiries that go quiet are simply lost.

Every unanswered enquiry can be a lost admission, and with it a year of fees.

The goal: no enquiry goes quiet without someone knowing.

How it works

From website form to admission, step by step.

Everything runs automatically in the background. Staff work from email, as they do today.

01
Usually within a minute in our tests

Fast reply to the parent

As soon as the enquiry arrives, the parent receives an email with the centre's address, a map link and phone number, so they know they've been heard.

02
At the same time

Centre alert

The centre gets an email with the parent's details and an "I'll call" button. One click marks the enquiry as taken and records when the centre took it, so head office can see how quickly every enquiry was picked up.

03
If nobody responds

Automatic escalation

Untouched enquiries move up from the centre to the city lead to head office, at time limits the school sets.

04
When a visit is booked

Visit confirmed, reminded and in the calendar

A booked visit must have a date. The parent gets a confirmation and a reminder, and the visit is added to the calendar.

05
All the time

One enquiry log for head office

Every enquiry, its status and its response time are recorded in a single SharePoint list across all centres. If anything in the system breaks, an alert goes out immediately.

Escalation

Nothing sits untouched.

Level 2 · e.g. after 2 hours

Alerted if the centre hasn't responded in time.

Alerted if the enquiry is still untouched.

We record how quickly enquiries are picked up for two weeks before go-live, then during the pilot, so you see the difference in your own numbers:

  • Median time from enquiry to the centre taking it
  • Share of enquiries picked up within your time limit
  • Visits booked per 100 enquiries

Questions

Common questions

Do centre staff need to install anything?

No. Staff receive emails and click a button when they take action. There's no new app or login.

Where is the data stored?

In the school's own Microsoft 365: the enquiry log is a SharePoint list, and emails go through the school's own Outlook.

Can we change the escalation time limits later?

Yes. The time limits and the people alerted at each level are settings the school controls.

What happens if something stops working?

Failure alerts are built in: if any step fails, an alert is sent immediately so it can be fixed before enquiries are missed.

Can we see it working before deciding?

Yes. A working demo is ready to walk through, and the pilot runs at one or two centres before any wider rollout.
