Welcome to **AwetomAItion** — a blog about putting AI and automation to work.

The name says it all: *awesome* + *AI* + *automation*. The goal is simple — share practical, real-world
examples that you can read in a few minutes and apply to your own projects.

## What you'll find here

- **Step-by-step guides** — building AI agents, copilots and automated workflows from scratch
- **Patterns and best practices** — approaches that hold up beyond the demo
- **Lessons learned** — honest write-ups of what worked and what didn't

## Finding your way around

Use the menu on the left to browse posts by **Categories** or **Tags**, see every post in **Archives**,
or check **Resources** for useful links. The search box at the top searches every post.

> New posts will appear on the home page. Subscribe with the RSS icon at the bottom of the sidebar to get
> them in your feed reader.
